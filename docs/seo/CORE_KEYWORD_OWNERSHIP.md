# Core Keyword Ownership

> **Updated:** 2026-08-13
> **Scope:** Valencia commercial owners in English and Spanish

## What ownership means

A primary owner is the page Rent&Roll most wants search engines to return for an
unmodified commercial query. It is not the only page allowed to mention, support
or rank for that subject.

- Category pages own broad customer needs.
- Family pages own the choice between meaningfully different products of one type.
- Product pages remain indexable and own exact model, size, capacity, format,
  compatibility and availability intent.
- Kits own multi-item stay scenarios.
- Blog and Discover pages own research and local planning intent.

Cannibalization is controlled by giving these pages different search jobs,
headings, supporting content and internal links. It is **not** controlled by
hiding valid products, stripping useful terminology or applying `noindex` to
commercial product pages.

An active product with a real Valencia offer and reviewed content should remain
indexable unless it is a duplicate, private utility or otherwise fails the normal
publication requirements. Secondary category membership changes discovery only;
the product keeps one URL, canonical, primary breadcrumb and inventory record.

## Family-page creation gate

A family owner is created only when all of the following are true:

1. the generic query has credible search, sales or strategic acquisition evidence;
2. the current products enable a useful customer choice;
3. the page can answer that selection job without relying on static inventory claims;
4. the parent and exact products can retain distinct, useful search roles;
5. English and Spanish content, canonical, hreflang, schema and internal links can
   launch together.

A fixed product-count rule is deliberately not used. Two genuinely different
products may justify a page when the query and selection job are strong; ten
near-identical products may not.

## Release and monitoring rule

Every owner release records the intended primary and supporting pages, keeps the
full parent catalogue available before optional guidance links, and verifies all
product routes remain indexable. Search Console query-by-page evidence should be
reviewed after the release window matures. Emerging product-page rankings are
protected when they serve a distinct modifier or convert well; ownership changes
are not made solely to force a theoretical hierarchy.
