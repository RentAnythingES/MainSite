# SEO Growth Audit — 7 September 2026

> Scope: core commercial clusters, product pages, blogs and Discover pages, with
> a focused mobility-scooter and wheelchair review. This is the first meaningful
> post-migration measurement baseline.

## Executive conclusion

Rent&Roll has moved beyond the “publish enough pages to be discovered” stage. The
new domain earned 199 clicks and 10,618 impressions in its first meaningful
Search Console window, while the live sitemap is healthy at 376 URLs. The next
gain should come from improving ownership, click-through and conversion on pages
that already rank—not from increasing the blog count on a schedule.

Mobility is the clearest opportunity. The broad mobility category has substantial
first-page exposure, but it is absorbing exact power-wheelchair demand that should
belong to the wheelchair family and product page. The scooter family page is
relevant, complete and backed by three active scooter options, but has not yet
earned meaningful exact-query visibility. Preserve the broad category, strengthen
the intended family/product owners, fix product-page conversion defects, and
measure enquiry outcomes before purchasing deeper inventory solely from SEO
signals.

## Evidence and maturity

- Search Console property: `sc-domain:rentandroll.com`.
- Meaningful observations: 7 August through 5 September 2026.
- Headline: 199 clicks, 10,618 impressions, 1.9% CTR, average position 13.6.
- Query/page tables examined: 611 query rows and 239 page rows.

- Audited mobility URLs: HTTP 200, self-canonical and indexable.
- Commercial evidence: 24 non-cancelled/non-refunded bookings in the production

- Migration warning: the previous 28-day period contains almost no new-domain
  history and is not a valid performance control. This audit is the baseline.

## Mobility & Accessibility priority

### Observed demand

- `/rental/mobility`: 10 clicks / 1,570 impressions / 0.6% CTR / position 10.1.
- Mobility query sample: five clicks / 1,506 impressions / weighted position 10.56.
- `electric wheelchair rental in valencia`: 1,153 impressions / position 10 /
  zero clicks.
- `wheelchair rental valencia`: five clicks / 16 impressions / 31.2% CTR /
  position 5.2.
- Prior Keyword Planner ranges: `mobility scooter hire valencia` 10–100 monthly;
  qualified broader EN/ES scooter terms 100–1K; local Spanish scooter term 0–10.
  Ranges were retrieved 8 August 2026 and are not forecasts.

### Current supply

- Three active scooters: foldable, standard and XL; one unit of online Valencia
  capacity each.
- One power wheelchair, one transport wheelchair and one rollator; one unit each.
- All six are active and `content_ready`.
- No mobility booking, quote or booking draft appears in the production database.
  Direct WhatsApp/email enquiries were outside that dataset at audit time.

### Page diagnosis

- The broad parent category is a protected winner and retains the general
  `mobility equipment` job.
- The scooter family owns scooter hire/rental comparison; the wheelchair family
  owns broad wheelchair rental; the power-wheelchair product owns exact electric
  and power-chair intent.
- Google currently sends almost all exact electric-wheelchair impressions to the
  parent category. The product receives only 30 page-level impressions and no
  clicks despite position 9.
- The family pages are well structured, factual, internally linked and in the
  sitemap. Their weak exposure is more consistent with authority/ownership lag
  than missing body copy.
- The parent category showed an unrelated stroller-and-bike trailer in its
  seven-product mobility grid through a secondary membership.
- The live product booking widget displayed invalid empty-date values (`NaN days`,
  `Invalid Date`, `€NaN`), a conversion defect prioritized ahead of more content.

### Approved sequence

1. Repair the booking widget's initial state and calendar-day duration math.
2. Remove the unrelated secondary mobility membership while preserving the
   product's baby-gear primary category and canonical URL.
3. Preserve `/rental/mobility`; do not redirect or replace it.
4. Strengthen focused family links from the accessibility guide and kit.
5. Improve the electric-wheelchair result promise without changing verified
   specifications or suitability constraints.
6. Record mobility enquiries and explicit loss reasons before deeper inventory
   purchases.
7. Recheck query-to-page ownership at Day 14 and Day 28; review qualified
   enquiries and bookings at Day 28 and Day 90.

Do not create a Gandia page merely because the query appeared 274 times. It needs
an approved fulfilment model and fresh market research first.

## Blog decision

No new mobility article is approved. The library already has high-ranking pages
with low CTR:

- family beaches: 11 clicks / 1,544 impressions / 0.7% CTR / position 7.5;
- Valencia with kids: five clicks / 346 impressions / 1.4% CTR / position 6.3;
- EN accessibility: two clicks / 235 impressions / 0.9% CTR / position 5.9;
- ES accessibility: five clicks / 81 impressions / 6.2% CTR / position 6.3.

Refresh the first three one at a time while protecting URLs and primary intent.
Preserve the Spanish accessibility result as the editorial benchmark. A new page
must have a repeated informational query, a distinct user job, a demonstrated SERP
gap and a non-cannibalizing route to its commercial owner.

## Inquiry and inventory measurement

Record every mobility enquiry with date/language, requested item, rental dates,
service area, landing/referrer when available, channel, outcome and loss reason.
Impressions justify improving acquisition. Repeated qualified, fulfillable lost
enquiries—especially `no_stock`—justify buying deeper inventory.

## External result-page observations

The 7 September Google scooter sample was led by specialist pages from Amigo 24,
Motion4rent and dedicated mobility providers. Rent&Roll's broad mobility hub was
visible lower on the first page and appeared in the AI overview, while the focused
scooter family page was not visible. The wheelchair sample was also dominated by
specialists and a strong local result with reviews. Bing's scooter first page did
not show Rent&Roll. These are point samples, not rank trackers, but reinforce the
need for exact-owner authority and real local proof.
